# External Reader test source

`qasper_2021.pdf` is the unchanged PDF from
https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.365.pdf (downloaded 2026-09-27).
SHA-256: `b15b4378988ed4e553dd312eaf334411abcf1fb28c53b846d360435eb55cd193`.

Attribution: Pradeep Dasigi, Kyle Lo, Iz Beltagy, Arman Cohan, Noah A. Smith,
Matt Gardner (2021), *A Dataset of Information-Seeking Questions and Answers
Anchored in Research Papers*, NAACL-HLT, pages 4599-4610. Association for
Computational Linguistics. DOI: https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2021.naacl-main.365.

The [ACL publication page](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.365/)
states that material published from 2016 is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The PDF was not edited. `../reader_real_manifest.jsonl` adds evaluation labels
transcribed by the assistant after visual review of rendered pages 1, 4 and 9.
Those labels still require independent review. No Reader predictions were used
to select these spans. This small development case is not a final held-out test
or a real-world scan benchmark.

The three earlier PDFs (`simple_text.pdf`, `two_columns_table.pdf`, `scanned_page.pdf`)
are synthetic fixtures generated by this project; see `../README.md`.
