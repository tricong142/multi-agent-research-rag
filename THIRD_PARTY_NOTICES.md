Check the source terms and provenance of each locally generated checkpoint before sharing it with others.

Recheck on 2026-09-26: the upstream [file tree](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jamescalam/ai-arxiv-chunked/tree/main) lists only `.gitattributes` and `train.jsonl`; its [community page](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jamescalam/ai-arxiv-chunked/discussions) shows a parquet-converter notification, not a permission grant. For a concrete corpus example, the [DistilBERT paper, arXiv:1910.01108v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.01108) links to the [arXiv non-exclusive distribution license](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/license.html). That license grants rights to arXiv; it is not a general redistribution license for this project. arXiv's [reuse guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/license/reuse.html) directs such requests to the author/rightsholder. This does not establish that every paper has the same license, nor resolve possible statutory exceptions. Permission for this corpus copy and derived indices remains unverified.
